A practical path

Business first. Property followed.

I started in business with Picture Perfect Aerial Installations, building a hands-on technical service operation across South East Queensland. It taught me how to create systems, lead people, solve practical problems and keep promises to customers.

That business also helped me move into commercial property. I was an owner and lessor before I became an agent, so I tend to look at property through the lens of asset performance, risk, structure and long-term value—not simply the next transaction.

In 2015, I co-founded The Commercial Guys. Today, as Principal Licensee and Director, my focus includes governance, compliance, operations and helping the business make complicated commercial-property work simpler.
