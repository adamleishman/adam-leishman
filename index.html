A practical path

Business first. Property followed.

My business background includes 23 years in trades and services, alongside ventures in air conditioning, clothing and promotional products. That practical experience shaped how I approach business: understand the details, build good systems and take responsibility for the outcome.

I was a commercial property owner before becoming an agent. In 2015, I founded The Commercial Guys, building a team and business focused on commercial property across South East Queensland. After 11 years, the business entered its next chapter through a successful merger with Sevenfold Commercial & Advisory in 2026.

My experience also includes Ozilite, a 30-year-old manufacturing business with a highly specialised product exported around the world.

Today, my interests bring together business, property, investing and emerging technology through Very Much Adam.
